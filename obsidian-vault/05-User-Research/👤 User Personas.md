## Primary Personas

### 1. The First-Time Traveler
**Name**: Priya, 24  
**Occupation**: Software Engineer  
**Location**: Bangalore

**Background**:
- First international trip for work conference
- Never applied for visa before
- Tech-savvy but anxious about documentation
- Budget-conscious

**Goals**:
- Get visa quickly and correctly
- Understand exactly what documents are needed
- Avoid mistakes that could delay application
- Track application progress

**Pain Points**:
- Overwhelmed by government websites
- Unsure about photo requirements
- Worried about missing documents
- Doesn't know realistic processing times

**How we help**:
- Step-by-step guided flow
- Clear document checklists
- Photo validation tool
- Realistic timeline expectations

## Design Implications

### For First-Time Users
- Progressive disclosure (don't overwhelm)
- Clear help text at every step
- Visual document examples
- Encouraging micro-copy

### For Power Users
- Quick actions and shortcuts
- Bulk operations
- Advanced filters
- Keyboard navigation

### Universal Needs
- [[🎨 Design System Hub|Portrait design]] warmth and approachability
- Clear status communication
- Mobile-responsive experience
- Accessible design
