## Design Principles

### 1. Warm & Personal
- Off-white canvas (#ffffff)
- Deep navy ink for text (#08304c)
- Soft pastel washes (mint, sky, peach)
- Polaroid-style photo cards

### 2. Restrained Color
- **Primary**: Portrait Ink (#08304c) for all text
- **Accent**: Rainbow gradient - ONLY for:
  - Sign up button border
  - One italicized word per headline
  - Small decorative strokes

### 3. Generous Rounding
- Cards: 24px
- Buttons: 28px (slightly rounder than cards)
- Tags: 9999px (full pills)
- Inputs: 16px

### 4. Soft Elevation
- Max 8% black opacity
- 3-6 thin layered shadows
- 1px oklab hairlines at 4-10% opacity

## Two-Voice Typography

### [[📝 Typography#Switzer|Switzer]]
**Role**: Universal body and UI
- Weights: 400, 500, 540, 600, 700
- Sizes: 10-36px
- Neutral, geometric, friendly

### [[📝 Typography#Basier Circle|Basier Circle]]
**Role**: Headlines only (31px+)
- Weights: 500, 600
- Sizes: 31, 39, 44, 49, 76px
- Dramatic negative tracking
