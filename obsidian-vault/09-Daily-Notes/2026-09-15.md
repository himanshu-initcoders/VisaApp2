## 💡 Ideas & Notes
- Question keys stay in the database (varchar 50) as UUIDs so applicant answers can still be stored against `question.key`
- Existing questions keep their current keys and flags on edit; only new questions get UUID keys
- Question categories live in `lib/question-categories.ts` (not a DB table); stored as `additional_questions.category`
- Question visibility is jsonb `additional_questions.visibility` (`sourceQuestionKey` + `equals` + `value`); helpers in `lib/question-visibility.ts`
- Document types live in `lib/document-types.ts`; `components_required.key` is UUID; `document_type` + `label` are the admin/applicant-facing fields
- Apply section flags: `visa_listings.show_general_info` / `show_trip_details` (default true); Additional Questions tab when listing has Form Builder questions
